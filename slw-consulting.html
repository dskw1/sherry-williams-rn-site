Always Evolving & Growing

Built on real clinical experience.

Teaching has always been how I process what I know. When I was diagnosed with Lupus in 2005, I built my own path back to health by doing what I do best as a nurse educator — researching, then turning what I learned into a curriculum I could actually follow. That instinct to teach is the same one behind this school.

This CNA program is built from two decades of hands-on labor and delivery, concierge, and postpartum nursing experience — taught by someone who uses these skills daily, not from a textbook alone.
