In 2004, I was diagnosed with Lupus. For an active nurse with three small children, news like that is crushing. I wanted answers. I wanted my health back.

I followed the plan my own doctors gave me, but honestly, it wasn't working. So I started doing my own research — I'm a nurse educator, I know how to build a curriculum, so I built my own path back to health. IV hydration, vitamin injections, and data-proven lifestyle changes helped place my Lupus into remission.

That's when it hit me: if this worked for me, it could work for other people too. Everything I've built since — every one of these four businesses — comes out of that same idea. Real nursing knowledge, made personal, and put directly into someone's hands.

“I built each of these around a moment in someone's life where the right kind of care changes everything.”
— Sherry Williams, RN, founder
